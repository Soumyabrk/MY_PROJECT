### Unstructured Data

**Definition:**
Unstructured data is data that **does not have a fixed or predefined format or structure**.

**Examples:**

* Images 📷
* Videos 🎥
* Audio 🎵
* Social media posts
* Emails
* PDF documents
* Text documents

**In simple words:**
👉 **Unstructured data = Data without a fixed format or organization.**
