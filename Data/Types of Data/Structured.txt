### Structured Data

**Definition:**
Structured data is data that is **organized in a fixed format**, usually in **rows and columns**, making it easy to store, search, and analyze.

**Examples:**

* Student records in an Excel sheet
* Customer details in a database
* Sales records

**Example:**

| Student ID | Name  | Age | Marks |
| ---------- | ----- | --: | ----: |
| 101        | Rahul |  20 |    85 |
| 102        | Priya |  21 |    90 |

**In simple words:**
👉 **Structured data = Organized data in a fixed format.**
