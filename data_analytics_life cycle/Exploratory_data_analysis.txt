### 5. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** is the process of **examining and understanding the data** to find patterns, trends, relationships, and unusual values before performing further analysis.

**Activities in EDA:**

* Understand the data structure
* Find patterns and trends
* Identify outliers
* Analyze relationships between variables
* Use charts and statistical methods

**Example:**
In sales data, we can analyze **which product has the highest sales, which month has the lowest sales, and which region performs best**.

**In short:**
👉 **EDA = Explore the data to understand what the data is telling us.**
