## Data Analytics Life Cycle

The **Data Analytics Life Cycle** is a step-by-step process used to turn raw data into useful insights and support business decision-making.

### 1. Requirement Gathering

**Requirement Gathering** is the **first step** of the Data Analytics Life Cycle.

In this step, we understand **what the business wants to know or solve** using data.

### What we do in Requirement Gathering:

* Understand the **business problem**
* Identify the **goals and objectives**
* Understand what **data is required**
* Identify the **stakeholders/users**
* Decide the expected **output or result**
* Define the **scope** of the project

### Example:

Suppose a company wants to know:

> **"Why are our sales decreasing?"**

During requirement gathering, we may ask:

* Which products have declining sales?
* Which regions have lower sales?
* Has the number of customers decreased?
* What time period should we analyze?
* Do we need sales, customer, product, and region data?

**Output:** A clear understanding of the problem, objectives, required data, and expected results.
