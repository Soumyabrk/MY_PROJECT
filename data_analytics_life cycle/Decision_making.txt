### 6. Decision Making

**Decision Making** is the process of **using the insights obtained from data analysis to make better business decisions**.

**Activities:**

* Interpret the analysis results
* Identify the best solution
* Make data-driven decisions
* Take appropriate business actions
* Monitor the results

**Example:**
If EDA shows that **sales are lowest in a particular region**, the company may decide to **increase marketing or offer discounts in that region**.

**In short:**
👉 **Decision Making = Use data insights to take the right action.**
